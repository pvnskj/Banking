Data quality rules

Credit-card repayments are classified as Payments, not spending. Money moved between your own accounts is classified as Transfers.

Refunds offset purchases. Tax refunds and investment credits are not counted as salary. Zelle, Venmo, Western Union and Remitly are marked as Fund Transfers and excluded from spending by default. Reclassify individual transactions as expenses when appropriate.

Two AT&T $699.89 transactions are retained as separate one-time phone purchases. Nissan auto lease is tracked as Car Payments, not Travel or Fuel. Savings ($48,734) is user-reported, not bank-synced.

The built-in overview provides previously calculated monthly summary amounts. After you import your raw statements, the app switches to transaction-based analysis rather than adding the imported data on top of the summary.
